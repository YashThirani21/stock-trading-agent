# Stock Trading Agent

A multi-agent stock trading system powered by GPT-4o-mini. An orchestrator coordinates four specialist agents to help you analyze markets, monitor news, manage risk, and execute trades on Alpaca paper trading.

## Specialist Agents

- **Market Analyst** — Retrieves stock prices, historical data, and fundamentals via yfinance
- **News Analyst** — Fetches financial news and sentiment analysis via Finnhub
- **Risk Manager** — Analyzes portfolio risk, enforces position limits, and manages your watchlist
- **Trader** — Executes buy, sell, and stop-loss orders on Alpaca paper trading

## Try These

- "Analyze AAPL stock and give me a summary"
- "What's the latest news on NVDA?"
- "Show my portfolio risk breakdown"
- "Buy 10 shares of MSFT"
